How it plays

David A. buys a bankrupt museum and finds a time machine in the basement. The rest is up to you.

  1. Night

    Take the lift to the basement and pick a year on the machine's map.

  2. 60 seconds

    Grab artifacts and time shards, fight the boss, get out before the timer hits zero. Die and your bag stays behind.

  3. Day

    Put the loot in showcases. Visitors pay to see it. Spend the money on new eras and upgrades.

      Installing

      The builds aren't signed, so your system will ask once whether you trust them.

      1. Unzip the download. Keep LastExhibit.exe and LastExhibit.pck in the same folder.
      2. Open LastExhibit.exe. If SmartScreen says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.
      3. It starts in fullscreen. Press Esc for the menu.
      1. Unzip the download and drag Last Exhibit.app into Applications.
      2. Right-click the app and choose Open, then Open again. On macOS 15 and later, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
      3. If macOS says the app is damaged, run this once in Terminal:
        xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Last Exhibit.app"
      1. Unzip the download. Keep LastExhibit.x86_64 and LastExhibit.pck together.
      2. Make it executable and run it:
        chmod +x LastExhibit.x86_64 && ./LastExhibit.x86_64
      3. To check the file, compare its checksum with the one on this page:
        sha256sum LastExhibit-linux-x86_64.zip

      FAQ

      Is it free?

      Yes. It's a school project by four students. Nothing to buy.

      Controller?

      Not yet. Keyboard and mouse only.

      Phone or tablet?

      The browser version needs a keyboard, so use a computer.

      German?

      The game is in English. This page is in both.

      It doesn't start.

      Check that the .pck file is next to the program, then ask in the Discord and say which system you're on.
